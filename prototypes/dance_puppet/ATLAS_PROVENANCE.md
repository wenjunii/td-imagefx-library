# V3 puppet atlas provenance

Created with the built-in image-generation tool in reference-image/variant mode, with `transparent_background=true`. The v1 fan and chain sprites were supplied respectively as identity/style references. These are AI-assisted interpretations of missing views, not exact source-art extractions or measured anatomy.

The generated PNGs are kept unchanged as local, ignored assets:

- `build/dance-puppet/fan-atlas-v3.png` (1254 × 1254 RGBA)
- `build/dance-puppet/chain-atlas-v3.png` (1254 × 1254 RGBA)

The 4 × 4 sheets contain front/profile/bowed/back heads and torsos, independent sleeves/forearms, lower robe, two shin views and one prop. `Atlas` samples calibrated gutters because the generated cells are not precisely equal. Left profiles mirror the right-profile art, an explicit 2.5D approximation. The bowed torso art is a foreshortened chest, not a complete painted top-of-back turnaround.

No source footage or generated art is included in the public repository. Regenerating a sheet requires recalibration; prompts do not guarantee pixel-identical assets.

## Exact prompts

### fan

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent animation-puppet texture atlas, NOT a composed character illustration. Reference image is the identity/style reference. Preserve its dark navy opaque fills, fine pale blue-white ink outlines, crown, facial identity and flowing Chinese robes. Create a SQUARE sheet in a STRICT 4-column by 4-row equal-cell grid. Exactly 16 isolated sprites, one centered in every cell, generous transparent padding between cells, no overlaps, no grid lines, no words, no numbers. Each sprite fills about 80% of its cell. Actual transparent background with NO outer glow, NO shadow, NO haze. The interior of each sprite is opaque dark navy, not transparent linework. Body parts have rounded complete hidden attachment ends so they can overlap cleanly when rigged. All longitudinal body pieces point DOWN with the attachment at top. ROW 1 left to right: (1) complete head plus crown and hair, straight front view, no shoulders; (2) same complete head, true right-facing profile, visible nose projecting to right, no shoulders; (3) same head looking DOWN in a deep forward bow, top of crown/back of head visible, no shoulders; (4) same head from the BACK, hair and back of crown only, no face. ROW 2: (1) neck-to-waist torso and belt FRONT, NO head, NO arms, NO legs; (2) same torso true right-facing PROFILE, NO head or limbs; (3) foreshortened bowed upper back and shoulders ending at belt, viewed during a deep forward bend, NO head or limbs; (4) torso and belt BACK view, NO head or limbs. ROW 3: (1) separate LEFT upper-arm large folded sleeve, shoulder attachment at top, elbow at bottom, NO hand; (2) matching RIGHT upper-arm sleeve; (3) LEFT lower forearm with cuff and hand pointing down, elbow attachment top, fingertips bottom; (4) matching RIGHT lower forearm and hand. ROW 4: (1) one long flowing lower-robe panel from belt attachment top down to hem, no torso, no feet, intricate folds; (2) one lower leg with ankle and foot facing front, knee attachment at top, foot bottom; (3) same lower leg and foot right-facing side view; (4) the separate prop specified below. Match the reference character closely. This is a practical separated cutout puppet rig sheet; do not draw any assembled full-body characters. Character: the tall, long-tongued fan-bearing spirit in the reference. Keep long tongue on front/profile head. Sandals on feet. Row 4 column 4: ONE open folding fan with ribs, pivot at center TOP, semicircle opening DOWN, no hand.
